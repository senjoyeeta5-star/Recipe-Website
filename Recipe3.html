Pancakes

Preparation time:20 minutes

Difficulty:Easy

Ingredients

  • 1 cup flour
  • 🥚1 egg
  • 🥛1 cup milk
  • Sugar
  • Baking Powder

Instructions

  1. Mix all ingredients into a smooth batter.
  2. Heat a pan and pour batter.
  3. Cook until bubbles form, then flip.
  4. Cook both sides until golden.
  5. Serve with butter and syrup on top of it.
