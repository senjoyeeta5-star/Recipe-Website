Chocolate Cake

Preparation time:50 minutes

Difficulty:Medium

Ingredients

  • 2 cups flour
  • 1 cup sugar
  • Cocoa powder
  • Baking powder
  • Baking Soda
  • 🥚Eggs
  • 🧈Butter

Instructions

  1. Mix dry ingredients.
  2. Add eggs and butter.
  3. Pour into baking pan.
  4. Bake at 180°C for 30-40 minutes.
  5. Cool and serve.
