Healthy Salad

Preparation time:10 minutes

Difficulty:Easy

Ingredients

  • 🥬Lettuces
  • 🍅Tomatoes
  • 🥒Cucumber
  • 🧅Onions
  • 🥦Brocoli
  • 🌽Corns
  • Olive oil
  • 🍋Lemon Juice

Instructions

  1. Wash and chop all vegetables.
  2. Mix in a bowl.
  3. Add olive oil and lemon juice.
  4. Toss well.
  5. Serve fresh.
