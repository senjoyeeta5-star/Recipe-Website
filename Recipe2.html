Chicken Biriyani

Preparation time:60 minutes

Difficulty:Medium

Ingredients

  • 2 cups basmati rice
  • 🍗500g chicken
  • 🧅Onions,🍅Tomatoes
  • Curd
  • Biryani Spices

Instructions

  1. Marinate chicken with curd and spices..
  2. Cook rice separately.
  3. Prepare masala with onions and tomatoes.
  4. Layer rice and chicken, then cook on low heat.
  5. Serve hot.
